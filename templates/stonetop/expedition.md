### A Stonetop Expedition — one-line tagline

*Recommended: 3–5 players. A one-session adventure. Target level 1.*

Write the whole expedition here in Markdown. Headings, **bold**, bulleted and
numbered lists, and tables all render on the card. Put stat blocks in fenced
code blocks so they keep their shape:

