# The Hound's Eyes
### A Stonetop Arcanum — ride a beast's senses

*Drop it on a body, in a barrow, or in the hands of a houndmaster.*

> Every dog within a mile turned its head. Not one of them made a sound.

---

## The Discovery
A whistle the length of a finger, carved from the throat-bone of a dog. It was found on a
drowned shepherd three valleys over — his flock scattered, his two hounds sitting vigil on the
bank long after the crows had come. The whistle makes no sound a person can hear. *fragile, magical*

**To learn its use, you need one of:**
- [ ] A houndmaster or shepherd who has heard the silent note and lived with what it does.
- [ ] To spend a full moon's turning bonded to one beast — feeding it, sleeping beside it, until it answers the whistle as kin.
- [ ] The drowned shepherd's shade, coaxed to teach you the trick that cost him everything.

## The Arcanum
```move
When you sound the silent whistle and fix your mind on a beast you have fed or befriended, roll +WIS:
10+ you ride its senses cleanly — see, hear, and smell through it, near or far, as long as you concentrate.
7-9 you ride it, but choose 1 (or roll d4):
1. Your own body slumps where you sit — blind, deaf, and helpless until you return.
2. The beast resists; you get only one sense, and only in snatches.
3. You drift too deep — its hunger, its fear, its urge to run start to feel like yours.
4. It bolts toward what it senses, and drags your attention with it.
6- you fall fully into the beast and cannot find the way back until it sleeps, or something wakes you hard.
```
**The price.** Each time you ride a beast, a little of it stays in you. Ride too long or too
often and the GM may ask, when you are tired or frightened or cornered: do you answer as a
person, or as the animal you have been?
