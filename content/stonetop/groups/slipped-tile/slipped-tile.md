  a finger''s width out of true. By the time you notice it, they have been gone a week.'
background: It began with a clerk. Twenty years ago a woman kept Jahalim's books at the Foundry and watched
  the same forty names slide from wage to advance to debt to owned, and understood that nobody in the
  Delve was ever going to be freed by a knife. She started with one stick. Then she taught a mason's girl
  which tiles to move, and a Keep's runner which drains ran where, and by the time anyone thought to look
  for a pattern there wasn't one to find; only a hundred small losses across five houses, none of them
  worth a war. They have never killed anyone. They have never needed to. What none of the younger ones
  know is how many of the Bosses' books she has copied rather than burned, and where those copies are
  kept.

method: patience, drains, and the mosaics; take the paper, never the purse, never a life.
faultLine: the young ones have worked out that a stolen ledger is worth more held over a Boss's head than
  burned. Half the guild wants to free the Delve. Half wants to own it.
