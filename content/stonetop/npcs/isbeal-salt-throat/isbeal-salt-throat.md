name: Isbeal Salt-Throat
summary: Ask her a question. The deep water answers, and it never lies.
description: 'Somewhere near thirty, raised in the Mire, built like a fen-walker and dressed like a scholar
  in whatever the order can spare. She wears her collar buttoned to the jaw in all weather and drinks
  constantly; a clay flask always on her belt, refilled a dozen times a day, sipped mid-sentence, and
  she is never not thirsty. Her voice is the thing you notice: wet, salt-scoured, low, as though she has
  been shouting into wind for a week. She is calm, precise, and unnervingly good company, and she answers
  questions completely, including the ones about herself. If you ask her what''s under the collar she
  will unbutton it and show you, without any drama at all, and then button it again and go on with what
  she was saying. That is somehow much worse than if she''d refused.'
background: 'Her mother drowned in Ferrier''s Fen when Isbeal was nineteen, and Isbeal, who had been raised
  on the Mire''s stories and knew exactly what she was doing, gave the body to the deep water rather than
  the pyre. She got what she asked for. She has been the Long Candle''s deepest and only first-hand source
  ever since: the thing in the water answers her, in a whisper she cannot make anyone else hear, and every
  answer she has ever brought back has proved true. She has never once lied to the order about the cost,
  and this is why half of them love her and half of them want her given to the fen-walkers. What she has
  not told them, because it did not seem verified enough for the book, is that she has begun to understand
  what the whispers are for: the answers are not payment. They are bait, and she is not the fish.'
drive: to get enough into the book, before she goes, that her mother's price bought something.
fear: the water. Genuinely, physically. She goes anyway, which is not the same as being brave.
secret: she has caught herself, twice now, considering what else in Marshedge is beloved enough to trade.
statBlock:
  tags:

  hp: 8

  special: breathes water; the deep answers her questions truthfully, and always a little worse than asked.
  instinct: to know, and to pay for it herself
  moves:
  - Answer honestly, including the part you did not want
  - Go into the water when no one sane would
  - Know a thing she has no way of knowing
  - Ask for something beloved, gently, as a suggestion, and then apologize
customMove:
  trigger: When you ask Isbeal to put a question to the deep water on your behalf,
  text: she'll do it, she thinks she owes the world that much, and she'll want something beloved to give
    it. Roll +WIS. On a 10+, she brings back a true and useful answer, and the price falls on her. On
    a 7–9, the answer is true and useful and incomplete in a way you will not discover until it matters,
    and the price falls on someone. On a 6–, she comes back with an answer to a question nobody asked,
    and the GM says who has been noticed.
relations:
- Which of you has already asked her something? What did she answer, and what did it cost, and did you
  ever find out who paid?
- One of you knew her before the Fen took her mother. Say what she was like, and what you didn't say to
  her at the burial.
- Which of you would hand her to the fen-walkers tomorrow? Does she know? (She does. She isn't angry about
  it.)

- title: The charter.

- title: She was never the source.
  text: Forty years of verified first-hand observation, and every verified thing came through one throat.

    in a cellar in the middle of the richest town in the Vicinity, and it is nearly finished.

The best way to hand your players real information about the Things Below — and make them regret asking. Drop her in alongside the Long Candle, or on her own at the edge of any deep water.
