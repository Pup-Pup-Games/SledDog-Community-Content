# The Apiary
### A Stonetop Steading Improvement — a row of skeps, and the small luck of bees

*A steading improvement for Stonetop. Drop it on the map between expeditions.*

> Mind the bees, tell them your news, and they'll mind us right back.

---

## What It Is
A sunny south bank at the edge of the garths, set out of the north wind, where a row of coiled-straw
skeps sits each in its own little niche. From spring to the first frost the bees work the Flats and the
Wood's edge, and the village reaps what they leave: honey to sweeten and trade, beeswax for candles and
sealing and salve, and the makings of mead for festival nights. The old keeper talks to them. Everyone
swears the bees listen.

## Requires
*All of the following:*
- [ ] A sheltered, sunny bank near the garths and flowers, out of the north wind. Add it to the map.
- [ ] Pulling Together to weave the skeps and raise their stand or bee-bole, requiring a month and
      straw and timber (Value 1).
- [ ] A swarm or two to hive — taken from a wild colony at the Great Wood's edge (which risks the Wood),
      bought from a traveler, or given by a neighbor.
- [ ] Someone with a calm, steady hand to read the hives and take the harvest, near full-time in season.

## When You Meet the Requirements
Increase **Fortunes** by 1, add "**the Apiary** (honey, wax, mead)" to the **Resources** list, and draw
the skeps on the map. Give the keeper a name.

## Henceforth
```move
When the Seasons Change and you take the harvest of honey and wax, roll +Fortunes:
10+ a rich take; gain Surplus, and the hives go into the next season strong.
7-9 you get your harvest, but choose 1 (or roll d4):
1. A skep fails to robbing, damp, or a poor queen. The colony dwindles and must be split or replaced.
2. The bees swarm and make for the Wood. Chase them into the trees to hive them, or lose the stock.
3. Someone is badly stung, or a child meddles, and the hives stay roused and angry for days.
4. The bees fall silent and cluster tight though the day is warm. They have heard something the people have not.
6- the GM says what robs, sickens, or drives off the hives, or worsens the list above.
```

**Wax and mead.** When you Trade & Barter honey, wax, or mead, or need beeswax for candles, sealing, or
salve, you have what you need.

**Upkeep.** Bees must keep their winter stores. When winter grips the land, leave them honey enough or
feed the hives **1 Surplus**; go too greedy and the colonies starve, and you must rehive from scratch in
spring.

## In Stonetop
- **The keeper** — old, unhurried, and half-believed to be a little fey. They "tell the bees" the
  village's news and its deaths, by custom, and warn that a hive untold will dwindle.
- **A swarm out of the Wood.** If the stock came from a wild colony at the treeline, one skep grows
  strange: honey too dark, too potent, that shows the drinker things. (Hooks into the Fae.)
- **Honey and wax for the Pavilion.** Festival mead and candle-wax for offerings make the Apiary a
  quiet thread into faith, omens, and the Pavilion of the Gods.
