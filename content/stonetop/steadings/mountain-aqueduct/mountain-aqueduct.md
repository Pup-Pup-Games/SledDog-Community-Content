# The Mountain Aqueduct
### A Stonetop Steading Improvement — constant water from the high country, on the Makers' old stone

*A steading improvement for Stonetop. Drop it on the map between expeditions.*

> The cistern's fine 'til it's dry. The mountains are never dry.

---

## What It Is
A channel of fitted stone running miles down out of the Foothills, fed by a cold spring high in the
heights and carried on a gravity-fall the whole way to Stonetop's cistern. The village did not cut it
from nothing; they found a half-collapsed Maker watercourse in the hills, cleared it, and woke it back
to life. Now it never stops. Through the driest summer, water comes to the hill on its own, day and
night, and runs in the gutters of a steading that used to ration every drop.

## Requires
*All of the following, in order:*
- [ ] A half-collapsed **Maker watercourse** found in the Foothills, and the cold spring at its head.
      Add the line, the spring, and the route to the map.
- [ ] **Right-of-way** for the land the channel crosses: a pact with the Hillfolk, or a negotiation
      played out in detail, for leave to dig and build through the high country.
- [ ] An **exceptional engineer or foreman** who can read Maker stonework and hold the grade true,
      near full-time for the whole work.
- [ ] **Pulling Together three times** to clear, reset, and seal the channel — a season and 1 Surplus
      each, with cut stone, lime, and timber across the work (Value 3 in all).

## When You Meet the Requirements
Increase **Prosperity** by 1, add "**the Aqueduct** (constant mountain water)" to the **Resources**
list, and draw the channel on the map. Give the engineer who raised it a name.

## Henceforth
```move
When the Seasons Change and the water runs to the hill, roll +Fortunes:
10+ it runs clean and constant; gain Surplus, and ignore the next drought or dry-summer fortune.
7-9 it runs, but choose 1 (or roll d4):
1. A reach has silted or cracked. Pull Together to clear it, or lose the gain this season.
2. A Foothills neighbor has tapped the line upstream. Trade & Barter to settle it, or it sours with the Hillfolk.
3. Freeze or rockfall in the heights stops the flow, and someone must walk the whole line to the spring to find why.
4. Something came *down* the old channel from the Maker works. The water tastes of iron and cold, and something is in the cistern.
6- the GM says what is cut, fouled, or seized in the hills, and the cistern is all you have again.
```

**Living water.** While the Aqueduct runs, the fields it irrigates and the fountains it feeds keep the
village hale: treat a dry season as a normal one, and you are never *lacking* for want of water.

**Upkeep.** Stone heaves and water freezes. Each **winter** the line consumes **1 Surplus** to keep it
sealed and flowing; let a season's repairs slide and a break drops it to no better than the cistern
until it is rebuilt. And the high country now holds your lifeline: the Hillfolk have a hand near the
steading's throat, and they know it.

## In Stonetop
- **The engineer** — an outsider, most likely, or one who studied the old stone at Gordin's Delve.
  They speak of the Makers' grade and joints with a reverence that unsettles the elders.
- **What sleeps at the spring.** The Makers built the source, and the Makers left things behind. A
  good water-year and a cold one down the channel are not always the same thing. (Hooks into the Things
  Below, and whatever the Makers walled away in the heights.)
- **A neighbor's leverage.** The pact that let you build can be renegotiated, broken, or dammed. When
  the Hillfolk want something from Stonetop, they need only point uphill.
