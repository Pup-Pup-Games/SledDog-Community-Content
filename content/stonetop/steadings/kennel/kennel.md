# The Kennel
### A Stonetop Steading Improvement — the hounds that guard the hill

*A steading improvement for Stonetop. Drop it on the map between expeditions.*

> A good dog is worth three watchmen, and never asks for whisky.

---

## What It Is
A low timber kennel and a fenced run, set a little apart from the houses where the wind carries
scent off the Flats. In it, a pack of hardy, deep-chested hounds — bred for the cold, the dark, and
the long hunt. They guard the hill, run down a quarry, and bark the first warning when something
comes out of the Wood that shouldn't.

## Requires
*All of the following:*
- [ ] A dry site for a kennel and a fenced run, a little apart from the homes. Add it to the map.
- [ ] Pulling Together to build the kennel and run, requiring a month and timber/supplies (Value 1).
- [ ] Breeding stock — a few hardy dogs or a litter of pups, gotten by trade with the Hillfolk, a
      gift, or a stray whelped at the Wood's edge.
- [ ] Someone with a way with animals to raise, feed, and train them — near enough to full-time.
- [ ] A steady source of scraps and meat to feed the pack (Stonetop's hunting and trapping serves).

## When You Meet the Requirements
Increase **Fortunes** by 1, add "**Hounds** (guarding, tracking, hunting)" to the **Resources** list,
and draw the kennel on the map. Give the houndmaster a name.

## Henceforth
```move
When the hounds raise the alarm in the dark, roll +Fortunes:
10+ they give ample warning *and* hold the intruder at bay until folk can arm.
7-9 they warn you, but choose 1 (or roll d4):
1. They rouse the whole steading over a fox, a shadow, nothing. Tempers fray.
2. A hound is hurt or killed driving it off; the pack is gun-shy for a season.
3. They corner it in the dark — and one of yours follows them out to see.
4. They cringe, hackles up, and *will not* face it. Whatever waits, dogs fear it.
6- the GM says what slips past in the dark, or worsens the list above.
```

**On the hunt.** When you take the hounds to track a quarry or run down game, you have **advantage**.

**Upkeep.** The pack eats. When winter grips the land, the kennel consumes **1 Surplus**. If it goes
unfed, the hounds scatter, starve, or turn feral — erase "Hounds" from the Resources list until you
build the pack back up.

## In Stonetop
- **The houndmaster** — patient, half-wild, closer to the dogs than to people. They know which
  hound won't lie, and which folk the pack distrusts.
- **A Wood-touched hound.** If the stock came from a stray out of the Great Wood, one pup grows
  strange: too clever, too quiet, watching the treeline like it's waiting. (Hooks into the Fae.)
